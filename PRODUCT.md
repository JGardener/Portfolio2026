# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and technical recruiters at product companies. They're scanning portfolios quickly on a laptop or desktop, usually with a tab-compare mental model. They want to see signal fast: can this person build real things, do they have taste, would I trust them with a production UI?

## Product Purpose

James Gardener's personal portfolio. Showcases creative frontend work (React, TypeScript, PixiJS) to land roles at product companies. Success means a hiring manager scrolls to the end, clicks a project link, and reaches out. The portfolio itself is a demonstration of the craft it claims to have.

## Brand Personality

Clean. Professional. Fluid. Animated. Smooth.

The voice is confident without being loud. Technical precision is the baseline; the animation and motion lift it above competent into memorable. Every interaction should feel considered, not showboaty.

## Anti-references

- Over-designed agency sites: Smooth.js scroll-jacking, neon glassmorphism, WebGL for its own sake. Style deployed without substance or restraint.
- Generic developer portfolio templates: Bootstrap/Gatsby starter look, hero photo, skill bars, timeline. Competent and forgettable.
- Bland SaaS landing pages: generic AI/SaaS cream-and-blue aesthetic. Looks like a product, not a person.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The portfolio is the work. If the code claims to build smooth interfaces, the interface must be smooth. No gap between claim and execution.
2. **Signal over noise.** Every element earns its place. Animation that doesn't communicate something gets cut.
3. **Technical craft is visible.** The custom cursor, the particle system, the canvas letterforms — these exist to show skill, not to decorate. Make the technical choices legible.
4. **Restraint as confidence.** The design knows when to stop. Not every section needs a dramatic entrance. White space and quiet moments make the active moments land harder.
5. **Alive, not frantic.** Interfaces should feel like they breathe. Motion is continuous and subtle at rest, responsive and precise on interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum; focus-visible states on all interactive elements.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected: transitions collapse to instant.
- Custom cursor hidden on touch devices (hover:none media query).
- Screen-reader-accessible structure: sr-only h1 on hero, semantic section labels.
